# Implementation Plan: R02-G15-02 — Voxel Origami: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `442-voxel-origami-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) |
**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `VoxelOrigamiCore` with the content pipeline, on the pattern of Moonlit Mechanism's (feature 432): the 100-entry
`VoxelOrigamiManifest` from the archived design (number, seed, band, budget, control emphasis); band profiles (cube
counts, scramble lengths, plan ranges); a constructive generator per manifest entry (a hinge tree with a collision-free
rest pose, a collision-free solution pose, orientations by control emphasis, the target as the solution's cells under a
drawn rotation of the group and a drawn shift, and a scramble of clear turns that gives the start); a breadth-first
solver over quarter-turn states (the exact case of the catalog's A-star) whose moves pass the kernel's collision and
exact sweep filter, counting turns tried against a bound of 300 000 and storing the states reached at every smaller
depth as the minimality proof; difficulty metrics; witness files with per-action state digests and the counterexample;
the catalog digest and the progression rule. Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md).
API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`VoxelOrigamiCore`. **Numbers**: at most 10 cubes in the catalog; turns tried bounded at 300 000 per candidate (the
prototype needed at most 3 670); states are arrays of quarter turns; plain `Int` arithmetic. **Seeds**: manifest seeds
through `SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_442. **Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02
--filter VoxelOrigami`; catalog certification in groups of 20 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (measured
locally, not asserted on CI); generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g15.*`;
no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests
with an independent depth-first enumeration), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work in parallel
against the contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests every agent
result and records checks only after every agent has reported.
