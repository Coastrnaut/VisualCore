# Implementation Plan: R02-G11-02 — Echo Cartographer: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `402-echo-cartographer-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) | **Date**:
2026-11-23 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `EchoCartographerCore` with the content pipeline on the pattern of Backwards Aurora's (feature 392): the
100-entry manifest with its provenance; band profiles (chamber sizes, wall chance, beacon counts, tolerance, miss cost,
witness pulse range); the inference solver (fitting placements counted by a pruned depth-first enumeration capped at
1 000, the greedy pulse plan from the opening knowledge, work counted and bounded at 400 000 nodes); the difficulty
metrics; the seeded generator (candidate c of entry n from `SeedDerivation.derive(plannedSeed, index: c)`, at most 256
candidates, `generation(n)`); the catalog digest with R02-F24's `PuzzleCatalog`; and the witness record (actions, echo
digests, placements after each pulse, play fingerprints, the counterexample one pulse short) with its 100 files under
`docs/games/echo-cartographer/levels/`. Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md).
API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`EchoCartographerCore`. **Numbers**: at most 14 × 14 = 196 cells, at most 4 beacons; enumeration nodes bounded at
400 000 per candidate; plain `Int` arithmetic. **Seeds**: manifest seeds through `SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed
2_402. **Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter EchoCartographer`; catalog
certification in groups of 5 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (measured locally, not asserted on CI);
generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g11.*`; no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests
with an independent brute force), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work in parallel against the
contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests every agent result and
records checks only after every agent has reported.
