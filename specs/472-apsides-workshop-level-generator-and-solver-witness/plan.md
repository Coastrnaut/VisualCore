# Implementation Plan: R02-G18-02 — Apsides Workshop: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `472-apsides-workshop-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) | **Date**:
2026-11-25 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `ApsidesWorkshopCore` with the content pipeline, on the pattern of Celestial Eclipse Lab's (feature 462): the
100-entry `ApsidesWorkshopManifest` from the archived design (number, seed, band, budget, control emphasis); band
profiles (periods, targets, apsis targets, sweep and apsis half-widths, planets, levers moved, plan range); a
constructive generator per manifest entry (a valid winning orbit, sweep targets from its shares and apsis targets from
its apsides with bands around them, ordered by event tick, planets placed beside its path, then one or both levers moved
away); a depth search solver over orbits by the number of levers changed (depth 1: every knob setting, then every size;
depth 2: every orbit changing both), judging each orbit by the kernel's report with the apsis targets checked first from
the orbit's steps, counting orbits judged against a bound of 16 000 and storing the orbits at every smaller depth as the
minimality proof; a witness order for two tunings that passes only valid orbits; difficulty metrics; witness files with
per-action state digests, the terminal report and the counterexample; the catalog digest, the neighbour rule and the
progression rule. The kernel is unchanged except two new error cases. Research: [research.md](research.md). Data:
[data-model.md](data-model.md). API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`ApsidesWorkshopCore`. **Numbers**: periods 24 … 48 in the catalog; at most 423 orbits at depth 1 and 13 880 at depth 2;
orbits judged bounded at 16 000 per candidate (the prototype needed at most 13 731); all orbit rules are the kernel's
integer rules (feature 471). **Seeds**: manifest seeds through `SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_472.
**Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter ApsidesWorkshop`; catalog certification in
groups of 10 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (the prototype built the whole catalog in 0.2 s; measured
locally, not asserted on CI); generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g18.*`;
no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests with
an independent enumeration through the kernel's full report, no apsis shortcut), B (witness and its tests) and C
(generator tests) then work in parallel against the contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and
the witness files, retests every agent result and records checks only after every agent has reported.
