
**App-owned sources** come later, in `Packages/ExperiencesCreative/Aurora/{Core,CoreTests,Experience,ExperienceTests}`
(A21-P01). Their dependency direction will be experience → VisualCore products (`VisualCoreRendering`,
`VisualCoreScenes`, `VisualCoreMath`, `VisualCoreSimulation`, `VisualCorePersistence`, `VisualCoreExperience`,
`VisualCoreExperienceHost`, `VisualCoreUI`, `VisualCoreDiagnostics`, `VisualCoreAudio`, `VisualCoreAudioDSP`), never
the reverse. There is no Swift, project or package change now.

**Lifecycle contracts**, inventoried and not changed:
- `RenderHost` owns EGL on the main thread; its `FrameScheduler` applies `FramePacing` (30 frames per second under Low
  Power Mode or a serious thermal state); `FrameInfo.time` is wall-clock, so the experience's rendered time comes from
  `SceneClock` through `fixedTime`.
- The experience host owns open, suspend, resume and close, preparation with cancellation, and memory warnings; sound
  stops on suspend.
- Scenes create GPU objects in setup and release them in teardown; `CrossfadeScene` and render targets are rebuilt
  from CPU state after a context loss or resize.

**Data schemas:** the contract JSON only. **Migration and rollback:** documents only; revert the commits.
