# Implementation Plan: R02-G08-02 — Faceted Forge: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `372-faceted-forge-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) | **Date**:
2026-11-22 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `FacetedForgeCore` with the content pipeline on the pattern of Amethyst Escape's (feature 362): the 100-entry
manifest with provenance, band profiles (crystal shape, cut-count range, directions), a seeded candidate generator, a
depth-first solver that finds a winning sequence of exactly P − 1 cuts from the crystal alone (the lower bound of
clarification Q2) with partition pruning and counted work, difficulty metrics, witness files with per-cut digests and
counterexamples, the catalog digest and the progression check; the kernel's ranges widen additively (budget 1 … 20,
target faces 4 … 34). Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md). API:
[contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`). No GPU or UI code; no new target.
**Numbers**: a level has at most 8 target pieces (7 cuts); a node of the search tries at most 8 pieces × 13 directions ×
the crossing depths; work is counted in cut evaluations (bound 20,000 per level, about 2.6 s of debug cutting).
**Seeds**: candidate k of entry n uses `SeedDerivation.derive(plannedSeed, index: k)`; fixtures seed 2_372.
**Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter FacetedForge`; catalog tests in groups of 5.
**Performance**: measured per group locally (under 10 s on a debug host build); not asserted on CI.
**Constraints**: additive; R02-G08-01's types unchanged except two new error cases and two widened ranges; witness
files written only with `FACETED_FORGE_WRITE_WITNESSES=1`.

No NEEDS CLARIFICATION remains.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the range widening, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator;
agents A (solver tests with an independent check), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work in
parallel against the contract; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests every agent result and
records checks only after every agent has reported.
