# Implementation Plan: R02-G14-02 — Moonlit Mechanism: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `432-moonlit-mechanism-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) |
**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `MoonlitMechanismCore` with the content pipeline, on the pattern of Acoustic Constellator's (feature 422): the
100-entry `MoonlitMechanismManifest` from the archived design; band profiles (gears, lamp heights, key frames,
scramble lengths, plan ranges); a constructive generator per manifest entry (a train with meshing divisors of at least
2, a solution set-up, blade patterns and slot tables, key frames whose targets are the solution's casts, forbidden
cells outside every cast, alignment values from the solution, and a scramble that gives the start); a solver that
finds the minimal plan vector (net turns, net lamp steps, net re-seats) by L1 norm in a fixed order, skipping vectors
that leave the lamp out of range or a mesh misaligned and judging the rest with the kernel's run, counting runs
against a bound of 400,000 and storing the count of smaller vectors tried as the minimality proof; difficulty metrics;
witness files with per-action state digests and the counterexample; the catalog digest and the progression rule.
Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md). API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md).
Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`MoonlitMechanismCore`. **Numbers**: at most 5 gears, so plan vectors have at most 6 coordinates; runs bounded at
400,000 per candidate (the prototype needed at most 760); plain `Int` arithmetic. **Seeds**: manifest seeds through
`SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_432. **Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter
MoonlitMechanism`; catalog certification in groups of 20 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (measured
locally, not asserted on CI); generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g14.*`;
no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests
with an independent breadth-first search), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work in parallel
against the contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests every agent
result and records checks only after every agent has reported.
