# Implementation Plan: R02-G16-02 — Starlit Cartouche: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `452-starlit-cartouche-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) |
**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `StarlitCartoucheCore` with the content pipeline, on the pattern of Voxel Origami's (feature 442): the 100-entry
`StarlitCartoucheManifest` from the archived design (number, seed, band, budget, control emphasis); band profiles (star
counts, route lengths, decoys, plan ranges); a constructive generator per manifest entry (stars on orbits with distinct
glyph points, a range, a random route of flyable legs whose corridors become the symbol, decoy corridors, hazards that
miss the route, windows tight for `time launch`, the deadline after the route's last arrival); a time-expanded
breadth-first solver over route states (star, beat, strokes drawn) whose legs pass the kernel's refusals and failures,
counting legs tried against a bound of 300 000 and storing the states reached at every smaller depth as the minimality
proof; difficulty metrics; witness files with per-action state digests and the counterexample; the catalog digest and
the progression rule; the kernel's budget bound widened to 1 … 24 for the manifest's budgets 8 … 19. Research:
[research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md). API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md).
Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`StarlitCartoucheCore`. **Numbers**: at most 10 stars in the catalog; legs tried bounded at 300 000 per candidate (the
prototype needed at most 33 832); states are (star, beat, stroke mask) triples; plain `Int` arithmetic. **Seeds**:
manifest seeds through `SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_452. **Testing**: `swift test --package-path
Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter StarlitCartouche`; catalog certification in groups of 10 levels, each under 10 s on
a debug host build (measured locally, not asserted on CI); generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**:
additive except the widened budget bound; reason codes `r02-g16.*`; no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the budget bound, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A
(solver tests with an independent depth-first enumeration), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work
in parallel against the contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests
every agent result and records checks only after every agent has reported.
