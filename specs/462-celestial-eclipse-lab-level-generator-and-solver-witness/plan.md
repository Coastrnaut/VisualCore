# Implementation Plan: R02-G17-02 — Celestial Eclipse Lab: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `462-celestial-eclipse-lab-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) |
**Date**: 2026-11-25 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `CelestialEclipseLabCore` with the content pipeline, on the pattern of Starlit Cartouche's (feature 452): the
100-entry `CelestialEclipseLabManifest` from the archived design (number, seed, band, budget, control emphasis); band
profiles (bodies, targets, horizon, bodies moved away, plan range); a constructive generator per manifest entry (bodies
on integer ellipse orbits in depth layers, winning phases, the target taken from the winning timeline's first events
with windows around them, then some movable bodies moved away from their winning phases); a phase-search solver over
settings by the number of phase changes, judging each setting with a pair-interval table built once per level and
merged in timeline order, counting settings judged against a bound of 60 000 and storing the settings at every smaller
depth as the minimality proof; difficulty metrics; witness files with per-action state digests, the terminal timeline
and the counterexample; the catalog digest, the neighbour rule and the progression rule. The kernel is unchanged except
two new error cases. Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md). API:
[contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`CelestialEclipseLabCore`. **Numbers**: at most 6 bodies, periods 4 … 8, horizon at most 48 in the catalog; settings
judged bounded at 60 000 per candidate (the prototype needed at most 10 291); orbit positions from a fixed integer table
of cos and sin × 1000 (no floating point). **Seeds**: manifest seeds through `SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_462.
**Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter CelestialEclipseLab`; catalog certification
in groups of 10 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (measured locally, not asserted on CI); generated levels
cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g17.*`; no clock in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests with
an independent enumeration through the kernel's whole-sky timeline), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests)
then work in parallel against the contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files,
retests every agent result and records checks only after every agent has reported.
