# Implementation Plan: R02-G12-02 — Harmonic Bridges: level generator and solver witness

**Branch**: `412-harmonic-bridges-level-generator-and-solver-witness` (spec directory only; work on `main`) | **Date**:
2026-11-23 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

## Summary

Extend `HarmonicBridgesCore` with the content pipeline on the pattern of Echo Cartographer's (feature 402): the
100-entry manifest with its provenance; band profiles (scrambled-count range, steps, tones, thresholds, decoys); the
minimal-plan solver (per-string bridge patterns over the window's steps at each peg, maximal non-contained options per
retuned string realised by their lowest peg, subsets of increasing size in lexicographic order with the product of
their options, work counted and bounded at 400 000 choices, the count of smaller choices as the minimality proof); the
difficulty metrics; the constructive seeded generator (candidate c of entry n from `SeedDerivation.derive(plannedSeed,
index: c)`, at most 256 candidates, `generation(n)`); the catalog digest with R02-F24's `PuzzleCatalog`; and the
witness record (actions, bridge digests, play fingerprints, the counterexample one tuning short) with its 100 files
under `docs/games/harmonic-bridges/levels/`. Research: [research.md](research.md). Data: [data-model.md](data-model.md).
API: [contracts/api.md](contracts/api.md). Quickstart: [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6. **Primary dependencies**: VisualCoreSimulation (`SeededRandom`, `SeedDerivation`,
`PuzzleCatalog`, `ProgressionRule`, `PuzzleDifficulty`) and VisualCoreMath (`StableHash`), already linked by
`HarmonicBridgesCore`. **Numbers**: at most 12 strings × 12 pegs × 4 steps of patterns; choices bounded at 400 000 per
candidate (the prototype needed at most 237); plain `Int` arithmetic. **Seeds**: manifest seeds through
`SeedDerivation`; test fixtures seed 2_412. **Testing**: `swift test --package-path Packages/ExperiencesRound02 --filter
HarmonicBridges`; catalog certification in groups of 5 levels, each under 10 s on a debug host build (measured locally,
not asserted on CI); generated levels cached per process. **Constraints**: additive; reason codes `r02-g12.*`; no clock
in any rule.

## Parallel work

The lead writes the error cases, the manifest, the solver, the difficulty and the generator; agents A (solver tests
with an independent brute force), B (witness and its tests) and C (generator tests) then work in parallel against the
contract in isolated copies; the lead writes the catalog tests and the witness files, retests every agent result and
records checks only after every agent has reported.
