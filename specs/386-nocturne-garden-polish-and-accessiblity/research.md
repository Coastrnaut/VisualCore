# Research: R02-G09-06

- **R1 Pure plans.** Decision: announcement and haptic plans are pure functions of the old and new core (and a refusal
  code), as in feature 376. Rationale: exact host tests; nothing timing-dependent.
- **R2 Fade only the lights.** Decision: ease the blooms' glow and the leak crosses; shadow cells follow the player's
  moves (as Faceted Forge's pieces do). Rationale: the flashing risk is in the glow and the failure mark; a move is a
  deliberate, paced player action. Alternatives: cross-fading every shadow cell (rejected: per-cell state for little
  gain).
- **R3 Sound from the garden.** Decision: the root follows the sun (110 Hz × 1, 9/8, 5/4, 4/3), one partial per planted
  bloom up to four, an octave when won, halved when failed; a loop length at which every partial has a whole number of
  cycles. Rationale: the sound reflects the state without being a puzzle aid; pure and testable.
- **R4 Panel layout.** Decision: as feature 376 — scrolling, Dynamic Type, wrapping rows, adjustable pickers, a summary
  element, at most 520 points wide on iPad.
