### Decision

**GO**

- A working host and touch path exist: RenderHost on OpenGL ES -> ANGLE -> Metal (main-actor) draws any RenderScene, and RenderHost.pointerHandler delivers multi-touch PointerEvents with stable ids and timestamps to ExperiencePresenter.pointer and cancelPointers, resampled to uniform spacing with a cap by PointerVelocityTracker, all public and tested (TouchAdapterTests, PointerPathTests, PointerCancelTests, PointerVelocityTrackerTests).
