### Decision

**GO**

- A working host exists: RenderHost on OpenGL ES -> ANGLE -> Metal (main-actor) draws any RenderScene inside setup, resize, render and teardown, with pause, resume and recreateContext, all public and tested (RenderHostTests, SessionLifecycleTests, ShellRenderingTests).
- A chain of passes over render targets in the host's context, one sampling another's output, exists: FieldPass.run with named sampler inputs and typed FieldUniforms into a RenderTarget or the framebuffer, PingPongTarget and ResourceRegistry.makeRenderTarget with pools (FieldPassTests, PingPongTargetTests, ResourceRegistryTests); Simulation Lab and Fluid Art Playground use it.
- Bounded generated fragment programs exist: ResourceRegistry.program compiles and links in the host's context with structured ShaderError diagnostics, and ShaderBundle.visualCoreLibrary supplies the vc/ noise, palette and colour functions (ShaderProgramTests, ShaderBundleTests). The stop condition of US4 scenario 2 is therefore not met.
- 9 of 32 rows are available with tests; all 23 non-available rows have owned app-local gaps: G-MODEL and G-TYPES in A23-P01; G-CANVAS in A23-P02; G-COMPILE, G-OPERATORS and G-BUDGET in A23-P03; G-LIBRARY in A23-P04; G-DEVICE in A23-P05; G-RELEASE in A23-P06. No upstream gap and no VisualCore change is planned (clarifications Q1 to Q5); EffectGraphDescription and EffectGraphValidator are not used because their pass set is closed and chain-only (research D5).
- No permission is needed (no import, no network). Visual Programming Lab stays unregistered (22 registered, A01-A22, candidates empty) until A23-P01 creates Packages/ExperiencesCreative/VisualProgrammingLab with its targets and first real rules.
