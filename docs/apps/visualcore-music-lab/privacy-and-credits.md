# VisualCore Music Lab — privacy and credits

VisualCore Music Lab is the experience `a06.visualcore-music-lab` of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009). It has no app
target, Info.plist or privacy manifest of its own: the app's declarations cover it. Feature 052 (A06-P06),
FR-A06-601 and FR-A06-604. Sources: `Packages/ExperiencesCreative/VisualCoreMusicLab` (below: ML) and the audio
products `VisualCoreAudio` (`Packages/VisualCoreRendering`) and `VisualCoreAudioDSP` (`Packages/VisualCore`).

## What is stored on the device

| What | Where | Written by | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saved presets | `preset-<uuid>.json` in the experience's own storage folder, `Application Support/Experiences/a06.visualcore-music-lab/` | `ML/Experience/MusicLabPresetStore.swift` through `ExperienceStorage.write` (atomic replace, quota of `StorageQuota`: at most 64 files and 16 MiB) | The preset the person saved under a name (`MusicPreset`, format 2): the id, the name, the scene, that scene's mapping choices (feature and strength per parameter), the palette and the saved time. No sound, no samples and no analysis values |
| Saved session state | `state.json` in the same folder | the app (`SavedStateStore`, `Apps/VisualCore/App/AppModel.swift`), not the experience | The session snapshot of the core (`MusicLabState`): the source kind, the demo seed and mode, the scene, the mappings per scene and the palette, so that the experience can resume. A picked file is remembered by its name only; no file contents and no sound |

Nothing else is persisted by the experience. The three built-in presets (Spectrum, Waveform rings, Pulse geometry)
are part of the code and are never written. Its sources write no named defaults values (`ExperienceStorage.setValue`
is not called), no other files and no `UserDefaults` values. Damaged files, files of a newer format and files whose
name does not match their id are counted and kept, never deleted or rewritten (`MusicLabPresetStore`). Removing the
app removes the folder.

## Sound

- **No sound is stored, recorded or sent.** The samples of every source go into a fixed-size ring buffer in memory
  (`SampleRingBuffer`, `VisualCoreAudioDSP`), are analysed once per drawn frame (level, peak, frequency bands, beat energy;
  `VisualCoreAudioDSP`) and drive the visuals; then they are overwritten. No audio file is written, no recorder or
  asset writer is used (check `ml-no-network`, T-A06-603) and presets hold no audio.
- **Demo signal.** Generated by code from a seed; it needs no permission and reads nothing.
- **Audio file.** The person picks a file with the system file importer. It is read in place with security-scoped,
  read-only access and decoded in memory; it is not copied into the app and never changed.
- **Microphone.** Used only while the person has chosen it as the source, and only after the system permission was
  granted. Choosing another source, closing the experience or sending the app to the background stops the input (the presenter stops the source on `didEnterBackground`)
  (`MusicLabSessionTests.testClosingStopsTheSource`). The capture session uses `.mixWithOthers`
  (`MicrophoneAudioSource.sessionOptions`), so music the person is already playing in other apps keeps playing. When
  access is denied or restricted, the demo signal, files, every scene, the mapping editor and the presets stay usable
  (`MusicLabDeniedUsabilityTests`), and "Open Settings" leads to the app's settings.

## The one permission

Music Lab uses the app's existing microphone usage string and adds no other (FR-A06-601). The app declares in
`project.yml` (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`):

> Listens to sound in the room to drive the visuals; audio stays on this device and is not recorded.

The app's other usage string, `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`, belongs to A01 (Generative Art Studio); Music Lab
does not use it.

## What is not done

- **No collection.** Nothing is sent anywhere by the experience; there is no upload, sync, sign-in or analytics.
- **No network.** The sources of the experience and of `VisualCoreAudio` contain no `URLSession`, `Network`, tracking,
  advertising, motion or location framework use and no audio recorder or asset writer (check `ml-no-network`,
  T-A06-603). The app tracks nothing (`NSPrivacyTracking` false, no tracking domains).
- **No capture of other apps.** Only the microphone, a picked file or the demo signal is analysed; the sound of other
  apps is never captured.
- **No other sensors.** The analysis detail follows the quality level the host sets (level 0 to 2); the host's
  `QualityGovernor` decides it from the thermal state and Low Power Mode (public `ProcessInfo` state). The experience
  reads neither itself.
- **No hearing or health statement.** The sound metrics text is labelled approximate and is a visual aid only.

## Required-reason APIs and the app's privacy manifest

The app manifest `Apps/VisualCore/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares no tracking, no collected data types and
three accessed-API categories. The experience's uses fall under them; it adds none:

| Category and reason | Use by Music Lab |
|---|---|
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryFileTimestamp`, `C617.1` | `ExperienceStorage` reads file attributes (`attributesOfItem`: type and size) of the experience's own files when the preset store lists, reads or writes presets |
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults`, `CA92.1` | `ExperienceStorage` holds the app's `UserDefaults` for named values under the prefix `exp.a06.visualcore-music-lab.`; Music Lab stores none |
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategorySystemBootTime`, `35F9.1` | Frame times and quality decisions come from the app's render host (`ExperienceSession`); the experience's sources read no boot time. Elapsed time inside the app only |

The built product is checked against this manifest by `release_check.py --only app-release-build
app-privacy-manifest app-no-tracking app-usage-descriptions` ([build-and-archive](../../release/build-and-archive.md)).

## Credits

- **Code.** The experience's sources, including the ring and mesh geometry of the scenes
  (`ML/Experience/MusicLabScenes.swift`, drawn through the host's renderers), and the audio products `VisualCoreAudio` and `VisualCoreAudioDSP` (ring buffer, spectrum transform, bands, beat
  energy) are this repository's own code. The experience depends only on the local packages `Packages/VisualCore` and
  `Packages/VisualCoreRendering` (`Packages/ExperiencesCreative/Package.swift`); it adds no third-party package. The
  app's third-party components (ANGLE and the libraries inside it, listed in `docs/legal/third-party.json`) are
  bundled under the app's `Licenses` folder, shown in the app's Acknowledgements and checked by `app-licenses`.
- **Assets.** None. Every scene, mesh, palette and the demo signal are generated by code; no third-party art, font,
  sound or data is used. The test audio (tones, silence, note sequences) is synthetic and generated by the tests
  themselves; the committed preset fixtures are resources of the test target only, and `scenario-manifest.json` is
  outside every target's path. No image, sound or model is bundled.
- **ProjectM.** Not used; no engine code, preset format or name of it is in the experience, and ProjectM types stay out
  of the app.
