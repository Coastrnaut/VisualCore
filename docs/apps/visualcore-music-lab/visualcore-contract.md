# VisualCore Music Lab — VisualCore contract (A06-P00)

Read this before editing audio or graphics integration of the experience `a06.visualcore-music-lab`. It holds the
evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00 (spec
`specs/046-ml-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json). Verify it
with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app visualcore-music-lab`, and regenerate the block below with `--render`.

VisualCore Music Lab is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. Generic audio
machinery goes to VisualCore:
- sources (microphone, local file, demo), the permission gate and interruption recovery (VisualCoreAudio);
- the ring buffer, RMS/peak, windowed FFT and bands, onset/beat energy, smoothing, the demo signal, the
  feature-to-parameter mapping and the analysis budget (VisualCoreAudioDSP);
- signal textures for full-screen shaders (VisualCoreRendering).

Presets, scene rules, mapping defaults, accessibility metric phrasing, copy and UI stay in the experience.

### Decision

**GO**

- The render host, shader path, effects graph, animation, presets, persistence, export, quality and accessibility exist with tests: 13 of 29 rows are available, and the three scenes can be drawn with PrimitiveRenderer and DepthMeshPass today.
- Audio is buildable inside VisualCore's planned products on public APIs (AVFAudio/AVFoundation for sources, permission and session events; Accelerate vDSP for the FFT): VisualCoreAudio already links AVFAudio and VisualCoreAudioDSP is a pure, host-testable target, so no second backend, fork, private API or networking is needed.
- All eleven gaps are additive and owned: G-AUDIOSOURCE, G-MICPERMISSION, G-FILESOURCE (VisualCoreAudio, P01), G-RINGBUFFER and G-DEMOSIGNAL (VisualCoreAudioDSP, P01), G-AUDIODSP (P02), G-AUDIOMAP and G-SIGNALTEXTURE (P03), G-INTERRUPTION (P04), G-AUDIOBUDGET and the app-local G-A11YMETRICS (P05).
- VisualCore Music Lab stays unregistered until A06-P01 creates Packages/ExperiencesAudio with its four targets and real domain rules, so the experience package check and the one app are unchanged.
- A06-P01 starts with G-RINGBUFFER, G-DEMOSIGNAL and the source interface, so the permission-free demo path works before the microphone path.
