# Molecular Explorer — VisualCore contract (A15-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, camera, picking, parsing or data integration of the experience
`a15.molecular-explorer`. It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the
stop/go decision of Phase 00 (spec `specs/109-me-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in
[`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json). Verify it with
`python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app molecular-explorer`, and regenerate the block below with `--render`.

Molecular Explorer is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's
3D vectors, matrices and quaternions, the orbit camera with its view bounds and rays, ray–sphere picking, the UV sphere
and the capped tube mesh (a two-point tube is a bond cylinder), the lit mesh renderer with depth, gesture recognizers,
accessibility planners, persistence with migration, experience storage and quality tiers. The element catalog, the
molecule domain, the strict XYZ parser, bond inference, the molecule scene, picking rules, display modes, the panel,
the curated library and the import are its own (gap register below).
