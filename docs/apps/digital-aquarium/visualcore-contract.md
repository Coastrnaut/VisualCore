# Digital Aquarium — VisualCore contract (A09-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, simulation or interaction integration of the experience `a09.digital-aquarium`. It
holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/067-da-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app digital-aquarium`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Digital Aquarium is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's
seeded fixed-step simulation, particles, batched 2D shapes, gestures, effect passes, storage and quality tiers.
Generic aquarium machinery goes to VisualCore:
- bounded steering forces with a bounded neighbour search for schools (VisualCoreSimulation).

The habitat document and species catalog, fish shapes and swim animation, feeding and decoration placement, depth
layers, bubbles and caustics, the spoken habitat description and the ambient low-motion mode with dimming stay in the
experience. Everything is drawn in code; the habitat is an illustrative display, not an animal-care simulator.
