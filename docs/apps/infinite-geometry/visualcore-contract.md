# Infinite Geometry — VisualCore contract (A05-P00)

Read this before editing graphics integration of the experience `a05.infinite-geometry`. It holds the evidence-backed
integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00 (spec
`specs/039-ig-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json). Verify it
with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app infinite-geometry`, and regenerate the block below with `--render`.

Infinite Geometry is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. Generic geometry
machinery goes to VisualCore:
- symmetry mappings (VisualCoreMath) and their GLSL fold with 2D SDFs (VisualCoreRendering);
- a depth-correct mesh scene (VisualCoreRendering) and orbit-camera bounds, pan and reset (VisualCoreMath);
- a bounded mesh cache (VisualCoreScenes) and tier-driven tessellation (VisualCoreDiagnostics).

Presets, tiling and composition rules, explanations, copy and UI stay in the experience.

### Decision

**GO**

- The render host, shader path, depth targets and pipelines (R02-F04) and the procedural mesh compiler with validation (R02-F05) exist with tests: 16 of 22 rows are available.
- All six gaps are additive and reusable and need no second renderer, fork, private API or networking: G-SYMMETRY (VisualCoreMath, P01), G-SDF2D (VisualCoreRendering, P02), G-MESHSCENE and G-ORBITBOUNDS (P03), G-MESHCACHE and G-MESHLOD (P05).
- Infinite Geometry stays unregistered until A05-P01 creates its four targets with real domain rules, so the experience package check and the one app are unchanged.
- A05-P01 starts with G-SYMMETRY and the GPU-free GeometryPreset domain.
