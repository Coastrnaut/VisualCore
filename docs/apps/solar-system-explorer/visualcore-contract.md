# Solar System Explorer — VisualCore contract (A14-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, camera, time or data integration of the experience `a14.solar-system-explorer`.
It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/102-sse-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app solar-system-explorer`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Solar System Explorer is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses
VisualCore's 3D vectors, matrices and quaternions, the orbit camera, camera rails and framing flights, origin rebasing
and precision advice, ray–sphere picking, the UV sphere, revolution and tube meshes, 2D noise, the lit mesh renderer
with fog and depth, render targets, the scene clock, gesture recognizers, accessibility planners and sinks, storage and
quality tiers. Generic machinery goes to VisualCore when reusable:
- approximate Kepler orbit propagation for a simulated date (VisualCoreMath, A14-P02).

The body catalog with sourced data, unit conversion and citations (A14-P01), procedural bodies, rings, the Sun,
starfield, orbit lines, labels, camera-relative positions, focus and the two scale modes (A14-P03), the simulated clock
with a bounded, labelled date range, captioned tours, fact cards and saved view state (A14-P04) and the provenance and
tolerance review (A14-P05) stay in the experience. Positions come from the CPU model only; everything is drawn in code;
the enhanced mode is never presented as to scale and no observing or navigation accuracy is claimed.
