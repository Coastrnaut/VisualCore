# Solar System Explorer — scientific review

Generated by `ScientificReview.markdown()` from the bundled orbit model, catalog and tours; a test checks that this file matches it. It records what the model is, how it compares with published events and where every number shown comes from.

## The model

Positions come from an approximate model: Kepler orbits from the planets' mean elements at epoch J2000.0 (E. M. Standish, JPL), held fixed, for dates from 1950 to 2050. They are not an ephemeris. In the enhanced view, sizes and distances are enlarged and not to scale.

Orbital elements: Keplerian Elements for Approximate Positions of the Major Planets, Table 1, E. M. Standish, NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory Solar System Dynamics (numerical facts, public domain) (ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html).

## Checks against published events

Each event is searched day by day within 10 days of its published date and refined to 0.01 day: an opposition is the day when the planet's and Earth's heliocentric ecliptic longitudes are equal, a perihelion the day of Earth's least distance from the Sun. The model's Earth is the Earth–Moon barycentre. The offset is the model's day minus the published day (noon UTC).
