# Light Maze — device campaign plan (A13-P06, feature 101)

These measurements need real devices and run only after `ImplementationLock-100` (CLAUDE.md rule 5), in the order
D1 → D2 → D3. Until then every row is pending; no pacing, thermal, memory or latency number is claimed anywhere.
Simulator results never stand in for these rows. Every row is a {device} task (T-A13-615; FR-A13-622). Light Maze has
one drawing tier (`level-0`), so the rows measure that tier.

| Row | Measurement | Device | Procedure | Threshold | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LM-D1 | Frame pacing on the largest boards | D1 reference iPhone, D2 and D3 reference iPads | Open level 15 (Grand tour) and level 20 (Prism maze), the 10 × 10 boards with the most tiles and, on level 20, prism branches; turn mirrors and prisms continuously for 60 s on each, with the solved border cue triggered at least once; record frame intervals (Instruments, Animation Hitches) | at least 95 out of every 100 frames on time on the phone; no hitch when the beam is rebuilt after a turn | pending device campaign |
| LM-D2 | Thermal state over 10 minutes | D1 reference iPhone | Level 20 with repeated turns, undos, hints and solves for 10 minutes; log `ProcessInfo.thermalState` each 30 s | never `.critical`; never `.serious` while the board is idle | pending device campaign |
| LM-D3 | Memory high-water mark across level switches | D1 reference iPhone, D2 and D3 reference iPads | Open all 20 levels in turn five times through the Level menu, leaving and reopening the experience once per cycle; record the memory footprint high-water mark (Instruments, Allocations and VM Tracker) and send one simulated memory warning mid-run | the footprint after cycle 5 within 5 out of every 100 parts of cycle 1; the memory warning drops the cached scene and the next frame draws the board | pending device campaign |
| LM-D4 | Touch latency of tap-to-turn, drag and two-finger rotation | D1 reference iPhone | Tap a mirror to select and turn it, drag a prism to a diagonal and twist two fingers over a selected tile, filmed with a high-speed camera; count frames from the touch to the turned tile and the new beam | the turned tile and the new beam appear within 3 frames of the touch that turns it | pending device campaign |
| LM-D5 | Haptic and sound feel of the success cue | D1 reference iPhone, D2 reference iPad | Solve levels 1 to 3 with the silent switch off and on, the system haptics switch on and off, and other sound playing; listen to the sweep and feel the haptic on each solve | one sweep and one haptic per solve; the sweep silenced by the silent switch and mixed with other sound; no haptic with haptics off; nothing repeated on later frames of the solved board | pending device campaign |
| LM-D6 | VoiceOver and the system accessibility audits | D1 reference iPhone, D2 and D3 reference iPads | The system accessibility audits at the default and the largest text size with `VC_RUN_AUDITS=1` (owner ruling of 2026-11-13) over levels 1, 16 and 20 with the Level menu open and closed; a VoiceOver pass over every control, Describe, Hint and the spoken beam changes; Reduce Motion and Reduce Flashing on and off | no audit issue in the Light Maze screens; every control labelled, operable and readable at the largest size; the board, hints and beam changes heard in words; the solved border still under Reduce Motion and Reduce Flashing | pending device campaign |
