# Light Maze — VisualCore contract (A13-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, beam, board or interaction integration of the experience `a13.light-maze`.
It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/095-lm-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app light-maze`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Light Maze is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's 2D
vectors, transforms and camera, primitives with hit testing, the primitive renderer, render targets, the effect graph
with blur, bloom, composite and the flash limiter, additive blending, gesture recognizers, accessibility planners and
sinks, storage, sound effects and quality tiers. Generic 2D machinery goes to VisualCore:
- a 2D ray cast against line segments and reflection about a normal (VisualCoreMath, A13-P01).

The tile board and optical rules with the bounce cap and cycle detection (A13-P01), beam rendering from the tracer's
exact segments and snapped drag and rotate (A13-P02), the 20 levels, progression, undo, restart and saved progress
(A13-P03), hints, the spoken board description, colour cues and success feedback (A13-P04) and the solvability checker
(A13-P05) stay in the experience. Beams come from the CPU tracer only; everything is drawn in code; no claims of
physical optics accuracy or learning outcomes are made.
