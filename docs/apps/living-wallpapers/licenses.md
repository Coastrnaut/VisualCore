# Living Wallpapers — licenses and provenance (A19-P01, feature 138)

Everything Living Wallpapers draws is code written for this repository. No source image, texture, font file, model,
sound, generated asset or third-party asset is bundled.

## Safe-area guides

The guides are design guides of this app, stated as fractions of the canvas and labelled approximate: a top band of
0.22 where the lock screen time may sit, a bottom band of 0.10, and side margins of 0.04. They are not taken from the
system and are not measured.

Nothing else is bundled, and nothing is downloaded.
