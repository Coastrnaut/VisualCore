### Decision

**GO**

- A working host and an offscreen render-and-read-back path exist: RenderHost on OpenGL ES -> ANGLE -> Metal draws any RenderScene (FullScreenShaderScene, FieldPass, EffectScene, CrossfadeScene, CachedScene), and RenderHost.renderOffscreen(width:height:time:) renders it at an output size independent of the screen on the host's own context, validates the size first, reads the pixels back top-down and restores the live scene, all public and tested (OffscreenExportTests, StillRenderTests, StillCaptureTests, CrossfadeSceneTests); StillImageEncoder writes an sRGB PNG, and five experiences already share such files through the system share sheet.
- 8 of 26 rows are available with tests: offscreen rendering, effects and compositing, GPU budget and memory warnings, lifecycle with resize and context loss, quality tiers, licenses, the experience host and project documents with migration.
- All 18 non-available rows have owned gaps: G-MODEL, G-TEMPLATES and the video feasibility decision G-VIDEO in A19-P01; the additive VisualCoreRendering encoders G-ENCODE (JPEG and HEIC tagged sRGB, verified by read-back), the additive VisualCoreExperienceHost still time G-STILLTIME and G-SCENES in A19-P02; G-EDITOR, G-HELP and G-A11Y in A19-P03; G-SAVE, G-EXPORTJOB and G-HISTORY in A19-P04; G-DEVICE in A19-P05; G-RELEASE in A19-P06. No upstream change is needed to start.
- The only permission is the existing add-only Photos string through the share sheet (photo-library-add, declared in A19-P04); no PhotoKit call, no read access, no network. Living Wallpapers stays unregistered (18 registered, A01-A18) until A19-P01 creates Packages/ExperiencesCreative/LivingWallpapers with its targets and first real rules.
