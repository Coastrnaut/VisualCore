# Tiny Worlds — VisualCore contract (A12-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, camera, terrain or interaction integration of the experience `a12.tiny-worlds`.
It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/088-tw-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app tiny-worlds`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Tiny Worlds is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's orbit
camera and its bounds, mesh builders, shapes, normals, bounds, validation, digests and caches, the mesh renderer with
one directional light and fog, the depth mesh pass, seeded random numbers, off-screen stills, storage and quality
tiers. Generic 3D machinery goes to VisualCore:
- seeded noise and a height-field terrain generator with seam-free normals (VisualCoreScenes, A12-P01);
- an optional per-vertex colour stream drawn by the mesh renderer and depth pass (VisualCoreRendering, A12-P02);
- ray intersection against boxes, spheres and triangles for picking (VisualCoreMath, A12-P02).

The world's camera policy, props and placement, the world light, the world document with thumbnails, the island,
asteroid garden and miniature city packs and the detail tier stay in the experience. Worlds are generated on the CPU
from a seed and a document; everything is drawn in code; no claims of scientific or geographic accuracy are made.
