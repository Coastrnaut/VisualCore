# Physics Playground — VisualCore contract (A11-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, simulation or interaction integration of the experience `a11.physics-playground`.
It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/081-pp-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app physics-playground`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Physics Playground is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's
seeded fixed-step clock and runner, step policy, batched 2D shapes, gesture recognizers, circle and capsule collision,
storage and quality tiers. Generic rigid-body machinery goes to VisualCore:
- box contacts, an impulse solver with restitution, friction and positional correction, and a broad phase
  (VisualCoreSimulation);
- spring constraints with bounded stiffness (VisualCoreSimulation).

The body and world model, the editor gestures, overlays and state panel, presets, saved experiments and stability caps
stay in the experience. The CPU solver is authoritative; everything is drawn in code; results are educational
approximations, never engineering or safety predictions.
