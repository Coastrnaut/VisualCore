# Audio Odyssey — privacy and credits

Audio Odyssey is the experience `a07.audio-odyssey` of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009). It has no app target,
Info.plist or privacy manifest of its own: the app's declarations cover it. Feature 059 (A07-P06), FR-A07-601 and
FR-A07-604. Sources: `Packages/ExperiencesCreative/AudioOdyssey` (below: AO) and the audio products `VisualCoreAudio`
(`Packages/VisualCoreRendering`) and `VisualCoreAudioDSP` (`Packages/VisualCore`).

## What is stored on the device

| What | Where | Written by | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saved journeys (presets) | `journey-<uuid>.json` in the experience's own storage folder, `Application Support/Experiences/a07.audio-odyssey/` | `AO/Experience/JourneyPresetStore.swift` through `ExperienceStorage.write` (atomic replace, quota of `StorageQuota`: at most 64 files and 16 MiB) | The journey the person saved under a name (`JourneyPreset`, format 1): the id, the name, the saved time, the world, the three per-world mappings (channel and sensitivity of the primary and the light response), the speed target (0.5 to 7 units per second), the easing and calm motion. No sound, no samples and no analysis values |
| Saved session state | `state.json` in the same folder | the app (`SavedStateStore`, `Apps/VisualCore/App/AppModel.swift`), not the experience | The session snapshot of the core (`JourneyState`, schema 3): the world, the distance flown, the seed, the speed target, pause, the source kind (demo, microphone or file), the easing, calm motion and the mappings, so that the journey can resume. A picked file is not remembered by its contents or address; after a restore the person chooses the file again. No sound |

Nothing else is persisted by the experience. The three built-in journeys (Deep tunnel, Rolling hills, Night stars) are
part of the code (`JourneyPreset.builtIns`) and are never written. Its sources write no named defaults values
(`ExperienceStorage.setValue` is not called), no other files and no `UserDefaults` values. Damaged files, files of a
newer format and files that claim a built-in's id are counted and kept, never deleted or rewritten
(`JourneyPresetStore`; `JourneyPresetStoreTests.testDamagedNewerAndBuiltInIDFilesAreCountedAndLeftByteIdentical`,
`JourneyPresetMigrationTests`). Removing the app removes the folder.

## Sound

- **No sound is stored, recorded or sent.** The samples of every source go into a fixed-size ring buffer in memory
  (`SampleRingBuffer`, `VisualCoreAudioDSP`), are analysed once per drawn frame into band energies, flux and onset
  (`FeatureExtractor`), folded into the journey's envelope (`AudioEnvelope`) and drive the worlds' responses; then they
  are overwritten. No audio file is written, no recorder or asset writer is used (check `ao-no-network`, T-A07-603)
  and journeys hold no audio.
- **Demo signal.** Generated by code; it is the first source, needs no permission and reads nothing.
- **Audio file.** The person picks a file with the system file importer (audio types only). It is read in place with
  security-scoped, read-only access and decoded in memory; it is not copied into the app and never changed. A file
  that cannot be decoded keeps the previous source and shows a message
  (`OdysseySourcePickerTests.testAnUndecodableFileKeepsTheSourceAndSaysWhy`).
- **Microphone.** Used only while the person has chosen it as the source, and only after the system permission was
  granted. Choosing another source, closing the experience or sending the app to the background stops the input (the
  presenter stops the source on `didEnterBackground` and in `teardown`). When access was never decided, the system is
  asked once and a refusal falls back to the demo signal
  (`OdysseySourcePickerTests.testNotDeterminedAsksOnceThenPlaysOrFallsBackToTheDemo`). When access is denied or
  restricted, the demo signal stays live, a plain message offers "Open Settings", and every world, the mapping editor
  and the journeys stay usable (`OdysseySourcePickerTests.testDeniedOrRestrictedShowsTheWayToSettingsAndKeepsTheDemo`,
  `OdysseyAudioTests.testDeniedAccessRefusesTheMicrophoneKeepsTheDemoAndShowsTheWayToSettings`).
- **Mixes with other audio.** The capture session of the shared microphone source uses `.mixWithOthers`
  (`MicrophoneAudioSource.sessionOptions`; `MicrophoneSourceTests.testSessionMixesWithOtherAudio`), so music the
  person is already playing in other apps keeps playing.
- **Audio never moves the camera.** Sound drives only the worlds' shapes and light, within fixed bounds and step
  limits (`WorldResponse`); the flight's speed is the person's choice.

## The one permission

Audio Odyssey uses the app's existing microphone usage string and adds no other (FR-A07-601). The app declares in
`project.yml` (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, checked by `Apps/VisualCore/Tests/Unit/BundleTests.swift`):

> Listens to sound in the room to drive the visuals; audio stays on this device and is not recorded.

The app's other usage string, `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`, belongs to A01 (Generative Art Studio); Audio
Odyssey does not use it.

## What is not done

- **No collection.** Nothing is sent anywhere by the experience; there is no upload, sync, accounts, sign-in or
  analytics.
- **No network.** The sources of the experience and of `VisualCoreAudio` contain no `URLSession`, `Network`, tracking,
  advertising, motion or location framework use and no audio recorder or asset writer (check `ao-no-network`,
  T-A07-603). The app tracks nothing (`NSPrivacyTracking` false, no tracking domains).
- **No capture of other apps.** Only the microphone, a picked file or the demo signal is analysed; the sound of other
  apps is never captured.
- **No other sensors.** The drawn detail follows the quality level the host sets (level 0 to 2: pieces ahead and
  behind, draw distance and stars per piece, `OdysseyQuality`); the host's `QualityGovernor` decides it from the
  thermal state and Low Power Mode (public `ProcessInfo` state). The experience reads neither itself.
- **No health or comfort statement.** Calm motion, the easing and the speed limits are settings with stated effects
  (a lower speed cap, no sideways sway, longer speed changes); no effect on well-being or motion sickness is claimed.

## Required-reason APIs and the app's privacy manifest

The app manifest `Apps/VisualCore/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares no tracking, no collected data types and
three accessed-API categories. The experience's uses fall under them; it adds none:

| Category and reason | Use by Audio Odyssey |
|---|---|
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryFileTimestamp`, `C617.1` | `ExperienceStorage` reads file attributes (`attributesOfItem`: type and size) of the experience's own files when the journey store lists, reads or writes journeys |
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults`, `CA92.1` | `ExperienceStorage` holds the app's `UserDefaults` for named values under the prefix `exp.a07.audio-odyssey.`; Audio Odyssey stores none |
| `NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategorySystemBootTime`, `35F9.1` | Frame times and quality decisions come from the app's render host (`ExperienceSession`); the experience's sources read no boot time. Elapsed time inside the app only |

The built product is checked against this manifest by `release_check.py --only app-release-build
app-privacy-manifest app-no-tracking app-usage-descriptions` ([build-and-archive](../../release/build-and-archive.md)).

  sound or data is used. The test audio (tones, pulses, silence) is synthetic and generated by the tests themselves; the

  target's path. No image, sound or model is bundled.
- **ProjectM.** Not used; no engine code, preset format or name of it is in the experience, and ProjectM types stay out
