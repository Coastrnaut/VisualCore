# Audio Odyssey — device campaign plan

**Status: nothing measured on a device.** Every result below is pending. Feature 059 (A07-P06), FR-A07-622 and
FR-A07-623, task T-A07-616 ({device}). Audio Odyssey (`a07.audio-odyssey`) is measured as a row of the one VisualCore
app's device campaign (Auto-Creator.md section 23), not as a separate app. Simulator results, including the streaming,
long-session, quality, source and announcement suites of A07-P02 to A07-P05 (`OdysseyStreamingTests`,
`OdysseyLongSessionTests`, `OdysseyQualityTests`, `OdysseySourcePickerTests`, `OdysseyAnnouncementTests`), the shared
microphone recovery suites of `VisualCoreAudio` (`AudioSessionEventTests`, `MicrophoneRecoveryTests`) and the
host-side median of piece generation (`WorldPiecesTests.medianGenerationTimeIsMeasured`), are development signals only
and never device evidence; their interruptions and route changes are simulated events.

## When and in which order

1. Not before the gate `ImplementationLock-100` is recorded: `python3 scripts/migration_ledger.py guard-device` must
   exit 0. No install, launch or test of the app on a physical device happens earlier.
2. The owner has provisioned signing, paired, trusted and unlocked the devices and answers every system prompt that
   needs a person (owner steps; otherwise the row is `BLOCKED_OWNER_ACTION`). The agent never taps a permission or
   trust prompt itself; the permission rows below are carried out by the owner on the device, following the
   procedure, while the agent records the app's side.
3. Order: **D1** (iPhone 15), then **D2** (iPad Pro 13-inch M5), then **D3** (iPad A16). **D3 stays on iPadOS 26.5
   and is never updated**; if it reports another version, its campaign is blocked and the owner is told.
4. The runner of the campaign, `scripts/run_device_campaign.py`, is planned for UA-F13 (Auto-Creator.md 23.3) and does
   not exist yet. Hardware identifiers go only to untracked private files.
5. Test audio is a synthetic tone and pulse sequence generated by code, played from a file picked in the experience
   or from a second device near the microphone; no recorded or third-party sound is used.
6. Every measurement uses short runs; only the thermal row lasts ten minutes, as the plan of the one app's campaign
   asks. Longer behaviour is bounded by arithmetic from the short runs, never by soak runs.

## Measurements

The host's quality tier is clamped to the levels 0 to 2 (`OdysseyPresenter.qualityLevel`, `OdysseyQuality`):
level 0 → 8 pieces ahead, 2 behind, draw distance 64 units, 48 stars per star-corridor piece; level 1 → 6, 2, 48, 29;
level 2 → 4, 1, 32, 17. These are the configured budgets, not measurements. At most two tunnel pieces are built per
frame (`TunnelStream`).
