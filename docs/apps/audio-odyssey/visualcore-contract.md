# Audio Odyssey — VisualCore contract (A07-P00)

Read this before editing audio or graphics integration of the experience `a07.audio-odyssey`. It holds the
evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00 (spec
`specs/053-ao-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json). Verify it
with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app audio-odyssey`, and regenerate the block below with `--render`.

Audio Odyssey is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses the audio
products built for A06 (VisualCoreAudio, VisualCoreAudioDSP) unchanged. Generic journey machinery goes to VisualCore:
- the camera rail with eased speed and comfort caps, and world-origin rebasing (VisualCoreMath);
- drawing through any view and projection with depth fog (VisualCoreRendering);
- streaming tunnel and terrain segments in a bounded pool, and the long-session budget (VisualCoreScenes).

Only the curated journeys, copy and interface stay in the experience.
