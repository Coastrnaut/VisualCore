# Neon Arcade — VisualCore contract (A10-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, simulation or interaction integration of the experience `a10.neon-arcade`. It
holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/074-na-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app neon-arcade`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Neon Arcade is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's
seeded fixed-step clock and runner, particles, batched 2D shapes, gestures, the bloom pass, haptic and announcement
planning, storage and quality tiers. Generic arcade machinery goes to VisualCore:
- 2D collision: circle and capsule overlap and swept tests for fast hazards (VisualCoreSimulation);
- synthesized sound-effect playback (VisualCoreAudio).

Neon Dodge's rules, input replay and lanes, the title/pause/game-over flow, steering sensitivity, local high scores and
glow readability caps stay in the experience. Everything is drawn and synthesized in code; no skill, reflex,
cognitive or health benefit is claimed.
