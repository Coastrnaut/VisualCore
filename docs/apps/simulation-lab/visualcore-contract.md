# Simulation Lab — VisualCore contract (A16-P00)

Read this before editing graphics, field passes, simulation rules or capture of the experience `a16.simulation-lab`.
It holds the evidence-backed integration matrix, the inventory, the gap register and the stop/go decision of Phase 00
(spec `specs/116-sl-p00-boundary-audit`). The data lives in [`visualcore-contract.json`](visualcore-contract.json).
Verify it with `python3 scripts/check_app_contract.py --app simulation-lab`, and regenerate the block below with
`--render`.

Simulation Lab is an experience of the one VisualCore app (ADR-0009), not a standalone app. It reuses VisualCore's
ping-pong render targets, field passes with their input-is-output refusal, the float-target probe with its RGBA8
fallback, the CPU field grid with storage precision, still capture, gesture recognizers, accessibility planners,
persistence with migration, experience storage and quality tiers. The simulation rules with their CPU references, the
GPU step shaders with their boundary rules, the interface, presets, the legend and validation are its own (the gap
register below names the phase of each).
