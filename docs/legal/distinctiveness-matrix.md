## Rule

Each experience is reduced to (core abstraction, player action, win rule): R03/R04 core abstraction, player action, win predicate; R02 core abstraction, core action, victory; A01-A25 launcher category, primary capability, "open-ended". Two experiences share a mechanic when their triples are equal, their player actions are equal, or the Jaccard similarity of their player-action words is at least 0.5 (lower case, words of three or more letters, stop words dropped from the comparison, a trailing "s" dropped). A shared core abstraction or win rule alone is not a shared mechanic. A shared mechanic is fixed by redesigning the later experience in queue order inside its concept (ADR-0009 D-16). The human distinctiveness review is an owner review and does not gate (D-17).
