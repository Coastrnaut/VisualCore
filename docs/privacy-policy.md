# Privacy Policy — VisualCore for iOS and iPadOS

Effective: 2026-11-18 (version 1.0). Applies to the VisualCore app for iPhone and iPad published by Robert Pancoast.

**Summary: VisualCore collects no data. Nothing you make or do in the app is sent anywhere. The app has no account, no
advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it works offline.**

## What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores stays on your device, in the app's own storage:

- **Your place in each experience.** When you leave an experience, the app keeps its current settings (for example
  the selected scene, colours and controls) so it opens where you left it.
- **What you choose to save inside an experience.** For example named scenes, bookmarks, gardens, wallpapers or Shader
  Studio projects. These are kept only when you save them, and you can rename or delete them in the experience.
- **Your app settings.** For example reduced motion, reduced flashing and the quality choice.

You can remove stored data at any time:
- in **Settings** in the app, which lists every experience with stored data and resets one or all of them;
- inside an experience, by deleting what you saved;
- by deleting the app, which removes all of its data.

## Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it, and works without it.

- **Microphone.** Used only by VisualCore Music Lab, Audio Odyssey and Oscilloscope Studio, and only after you allow
  it, to make the visuals follow the sound in the room. The sound is analysed in memory while you use the experience;
  it is never recorded, stored or sent.
- **Photos (add only).** Used only when you choose Save Image in Generative Art Studio, Living Wallpapers or Digital
  Zen Garden, to add that image to your photo library. The app cannot see or read your photo library.

## What the app does not do

- It sends no data over the network and has no server. It downloads no code or content.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal information, usage data, identifiers or diagnostics.
- It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking frameworks and does not use the advertising identifier.
- It has no account and no sign-in.
- Shader Studio compiles the shaders you write on your device's graphics processor; GPU code is not a sandbox, and the
  shaders stay on your device.

## Purchases

Version 1.0 offers no in-app purchase. If a future version offers one, it will use Apple's App Store, and the app
will receive only Apple's confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

## Children

The app is rated 4+, collects no data from anyone, and contains no advertising.
